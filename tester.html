Sky Garden Founders

Founding Gardeners only

Welcome, founder

You are one of the very first people to play Sky Garden. You play the real game, the same way everyone will. The difference is that you help shape it.

How to start

  1. Tap the Start playing button on this page. Your tester code is already in it.
  2. Tap Play and make your character.
  3. A "Welcome, founder!" card appears. That means it worked.

Already played Sky Garden on this phone or computer? Tap Start playing anyway. Your progress stays.

Each code works for one player. Please don't share yours.

Tester missions

Tap the ✦ Missions button at the top of the game. These are things we'd love you to try. Each one pays 50 coins in the game. Play however you like. They're only ideas.

  • Catch a fish off a dock
  • Plant a whole row
  • Choose what to sell at the crate
  • Sleep under the stars
  • Build something on your island
  • Learn what a neighbor loves
  • And more as new things unlock

When you finish a mission, the game asks one quick question: loved it, okay, or confusing. One tap is a huge help.

New things unlock each day

Sky Garden opens up a little more every day you play. Come back tomorrow and a "New today" card shows what is new. You don't have to play every day. Nothing is missed.

Tell us anything

Tap the pink Feedback button in the game anytime. Tell us what you loved, where you got stuck, or what felt boring. It goes straight to the people making Sky Garden.

Something broke? Tell us what you were doing right before it happened. That helps us find it fast.

Your privacy

We save your game so it is there when you come back. Your name is not shown to other players.
